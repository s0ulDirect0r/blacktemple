one glorious day
deep in the jungle
in a cozy silk bed
a spider hatches
different from its brothers and sisters
tinted a strange black and red
despite its odd color
like all her children
he was beloved by his mother
what does life have in store
for our curious friend's tour?

our spiderling friend
ready for his destiny to begin
raises his butt toward heaven
spinnerets primed
he can feel the wind's draw
it is time!
strong, luxurious silk flows out and upwards
the breath of the jungle hits the thread
and away our friend flies
from his homestead

magic spiderling
bearing the red spiral rune
flies high, far, and fast
so much further
than one would reasonably assume
looking back his siblings are already landed
but him?
his horizons
are rapidly being expanded
he'll land soon
on the canopy
under the light of the moon

alone and isolated
under dark sky and bright moon
our soft squishy spider friend
starts to moltt
it hurts
its weird
our friend is full of fear
exoskeleton emerging
all of a sudden
it experiences holy energy
light and nurturing
Luna speaks clear
'my young friend, i am here'

'i know this feels wrong
you are so far from home
trust me when i say
you will survive this day
when you have molted
as many times as you have limbs
you will understand what you are
and i promise youll find yourself
in love's arms once more'
the goddess address the spiderling

KRAKOOM
a storm begins bringing rain
again our friend has just molted
shivering and in pain
the giant drops will flood and kill
if he does not summon his will
so by activating natural spinners
the spiral spiderling
weaves and shimmies
its way down from the canopy!

in the hollow of a tree
safe from the storm
feeling like it can rest easy
our spiderling homie
continues to evolve
into it's natural adult form
it ponders it's journey
it contemplates the moon
it watches it's body harden and strengthen
another molting completed all too soon

the storm has passed
some more strength
our spiderling has amassed
thrice more it has molted
while it waited out the tempest
it's body grows larger
the rune on its back grows brighter
oh? what's that i hear?
caw! caw!
oh no, that's a bird, i fear.
the spiderling must depart.

moving as fast as its little legs can go
the spiderling rapidly departs the hollow
why leave such a safe cozy space?
because my friend,
in there, he cannot escape!
a bird's beak has quite a reach
and energy to scratch and scrape
weave a thread
head out into night instead

swiftly with unnatural skill
this spider youth
spins and shimmies
on its thread
escaping just as the bird turns its head
in pain, another molt coming on
determined, our spiderling friend
scurries down a branch,
and leaps down into a mysterious tunnel
what awaits him now?

our hero
blind with pain
awakens disoriented
carried down the tunnel lane
by an escort of ants
dutifully making their way
his body convulses, final molting begun
these poor creatures
have picked up something
that maybe has predatory features...

tossed down in a pile of favors
our spiderling friend finds itself in the chamber
of the royal ant queen, whose mouth waters
curious about this strange spider's flavor
this timing is less than ideal
because the fated hour is here
ARGH
our spider friend molts
for
the
8th
time

BANG!!! A BRIGHT RED FLASH
the ants and their hungry queen
blinded and confused for being so rash
snapping, cracking, pulsing, smacking
our spiderling emerges from the remains of its youth
a full grown spider
hungry, powerful
in touch with its truth
Luna smiles from heaven

the ants realize
that they done fucked up
what they thought was a light snack
was to be their ultimate doom!
the mature spider with the spiraling red rune
flexes its spinneret
and with webbing floods the room
the ants are all caught, stuck and helpless
our hero hungers!

empowered
with a satiated belly
a full grown spider emerges
from the remains of the mound
smiling and happy
our spider homie witnesses something crazy happen!
a young spider lady, fleeing cruel scorpions
in our hero's soul
something pulses
he spins a kite
and flies to her!

loved by the wind
our spider soars
above the pursuing monsters
its kite released
this badass arachnid
lands on top of a pursuing scorpion
a scorpion,
dumb as a brick
attempts to sting it
but our friend has sped on
the stinger pierces its own heart
dead now
the game is on

lady spider with a familiar sigil
a blue crescent, beautiful and shining
swoons at the courage of the spiral champion
two scorpions remain
completely enraged
our hero confident
glowing red
its fires off a magical, forked thread
that slices and cuts off
another scorpion head!

one scorpion left
big and mad
it too bears a sigil!
yellow, circular, and rad
it points its stinger
and a beam of golden venom
attempts to burn the spider in red!
using all 8 legs he rolls out the way
weaving and threading a trap
with great attention to detail i must say

our spider brother
dances all around
this hungry scorpion, angry and proud
bolt after bolt of deadly venom laser
attempts to fry and pierce
our lovable spider skirt-chaser
the lady spider giggles and smiles
deeply impressed
by our hero's abundant wiles

the spiral on our friend's back
shines brighter than ever before!
our spider's skillful trap is ready
the pin ready to be pulled
the scorpion, angry beyond belief
fires one more time at our homie
who stands in front of a tree...

ZZZZZAP
the golden beam strikes the wood
piercing clean through!
the tree groans,
felled by the scorpion's unholy magical might
our spider homie smiles and laughs, his trap a success
the scorpion tries to move out of the way
but a spiraling red web, denies his escape!

NOOOOOOOOOOOOO
the golden scorpion screams
the tree comes down hard
crushing it into paste
shaking the jungle floor
alerting all
that our spider homie don't play!
now that they are officially acquainted
two magic spiders leave
to go on a date :)

what a wonderful time it was
full of love, action, and play
our grown, mature hero
made this lunar spider lass' day!
the two decide to make it a thing
so together they weave their web
threads glowing red and blue
their home glows with love
as we reach the moment of truth

in a moment of privacy
surely they deserve it
they continued the cycle of life
as all spiders know it
a powerful egg sac was crafted
and filled to the brim
from the union between them
the game completed
it's time for the reveal
the moon shines
to let us know what's real

under the light of Luna
a prophecy is completed
a man, proud and black,
covered in spiraling red runes
stands together with his lover,
glowing with the soft blue light of the moon
they remember the game they were playing
to see if again they would meet

the god,
sometimes spider,
sometimes man
smirks,
as if it was obvious
a conclusion foregone
'my lady.
we'll always combine.
how could i ever lose
an ass so fine'
she punches him
'you're so crude, my beloved husband.'

they kiss and they laugh
how lucky they are
to live so wild and fast.
they look into each others' eyes
and renew their vows
'forever, i'm yours'
they say at the same time

The End.
