## Introduction

I had a very turbulent 2021. Like many people, for that matter.

One way I dealt with it, was through creativity and community. I'm pretty proud of the creative adventure I went on. For a couple of months, I prepared and performed every week, a 3 minute long 'poetic invocation' of an aspect of my personality, or later on, an emotion. This was an amazing experience and I learned so much about what it means to me to express my character, and to express my relationship to topics like Love, Devotion, and Confidence.

I took a break for a while after that, and went on a rabbithole into Twitter, motivated by my newfound interest in web3 which was starting to blow up at the time. I'd never really...PLAYED, EXPLORED in Twitter before, but this time was different, as I was very intentional about who I followed and engaged with, because frankly I wanted to be inspired. I heard about an account called @visakanv from a friend of mine, and started following him and his ideas. One thing he shared about was the power of doing '100 things', and to that end there was a tradition in that part of twitter of writing a 100 tweet thread once a year.

The idea of that kind of challenge amused and intrigued me, and I decided I would write 100 poems over 100 tweets.

And...well, I just did it. Every day, I'd wake up, and at some point in the day, either at the library, or our living room, or wherever I found myself, I'd knock out as many poems as flowed through me over about 25 minutes. Sometimes I'd go way past that time box and just keep going until some signal inside of me told me to stop.

For 2 weeks I added to this chain of poems, with consistency and great joy, sometimes finding myself a bit ecstatic as I let the words flow into the thread.

The best part was watching how it evolved over that course of time. It started out with every poem just representing how I felt or something silly...but as I kept going I watched a narrative form, about me, finding my voice, finding a sense of creative flow as I weaved the thread. One night during that time period, the muse whispered to me 'this work is called Poesis', and so it was, and I began to share poems about that concept and word.

Eventually it became this extended narrative poem about a magic-wielding, poetic, space pirate captain journeying with his crew on a spaceship named Poesis, to a planet called Akasha to search for the source of creative power, and to win the heart of the Muse in the process.

It was so fun, it was so strange watching this thread evolve and choose a shape and let itself become it.

My main takeaway from this experience is...haha, well, sometimes, it's best to just let go of the wheel and let the muse, inspiration, whatever it is, just draw me into her Self, and see what happens, and love the journey, and the work that emerges from it.

I can't honestly say whether it's 'good' or not. It's silly. And honest. But...it flowed through me, and this imagination, and these typing fingers.

It deserves to live, and be seen, and read, just by the virtue of having been born.

Here it is. Here I am.
